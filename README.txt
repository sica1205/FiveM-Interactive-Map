# FiveM Interactive Map

An interactive GTA V map tool for creating, customizing, and sharing checkpoint-based maps for FiveM quests, collectibles, hidden locations, and custom activities.

Built with HTML, CSS, JavaScript, and Leaflet.js. No account, database, or server required.

## Features

- **Interactive GTA V Map** — Explore Los Santos and Blaine County with zoom and navigation controls.
- **Custom Checkpoints** — Create up to 500 checkpoints per map, each with its own name, description, and color.
- **Map Editor** — Add, edit, and delete checkpoints.
- **Import & Export** — Save your custom maps as `.txt` files and share them with others.
- **Progress Tracking** — Mark checkpoints as completed when exploring an imported map.
- **Automatic Local Saving** — Recover unfinished maps and saved checkpoint progress after refreshing or reopening the website.
- **Color Memory** — Automatically remembers your last selected checkpoint color.
- **No Account Required** — Everything runs directly in your browser.

## How to Use

### Create a Map

1. Open the website and select **Create Map**.
2. Give your map a name.
3. Click on the map to add checkpoints.
4. Customize each checkpoint with a name, description, and color.
5. Export your finished map as a `.txt` file.

Your unfinished map is automatically saved locally, allowing you to continue later on the same browser.

### Upload a Map

1. Select **Upload Map** from the main menu.
2. Choose a previously exported `.txt` map file.
3. Explore the checkpoints and mark locations as completed.
4. Your progress is saved automatically in your browser.

### Preview the Map

Select **Preview Only** to explore the GTA V map without creating or importing checkpoints.

## Important Notes

- Maps and progress are stored locally in your browser.
- Local storage does not synchronize between devices.
- Clearing browser data may remove saved progress and unfinished maps.
- Export your maps regularly to keep a backup.
- The tool is designed for GTA V / FiveM communities but is not affiliated with Rockstar Games or Cfx.re.
